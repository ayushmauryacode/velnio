A simple process from idea to website

Four clear steps, from the first message to a live website your customers can find.

01

Tell us about your business

You share what your business does, the services you offer, and what you'd like the website to help with. This can be as simple as a WhatsApp message or a quick call.

02

We build

We design and build a clean website around your business, using the details, photos and text you've shared. You don't need to manage any technical setup during this stage.

03

You review

We share the website with you to look through. If anything needs changing — wording, layout or images — you tell us and we adjust it.

04

Go live

Once everything looks right to you, your website is ready to go online for customers to find and contact you through.

What we need from you

Only what actually helps us build your website faster — nothing here is required to get started.

  • Business name
  • Logo (if you have one)
  • Photos of your business, if available
  • List of services
  • A short business description
  • Contact information
  • Business address, if relevant
  • Social links, if you use any
